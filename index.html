Good to know

Anything else? Ask us on WhatsApp.

How do the online classes work?

Classes are live on video. Dr. Seema watches as you practise and corrects your posture and breathing in real time, so every asana is done safely.

I'm a complete beginner. Can I join?

Yes. Classes start with gentle joint-loosening (Sukshma Vyayama) and build up step by step, so beginners and experienced students can practise together.

Can Dr. Seema help with a specific health condition?

Yes. She combines Ayurvedic assessment with therapeutic yoga, and often works with back stiffness, neck pain, stress, digestive issues and metabolic conditions. Yoga complements medical care, so please keep your doctor informed.

Which language are classes taught in?

Hindi and English.

Can I take private one-on-one sessions?

Yes. Alongside the small morning group batches, you can book private one-on-one sessions.

What are the class timings?

Hour-long batches run from 6:30 to 10:30 AM IST on weekdays, and from 7:30 to 10:30 AM IST on Saturday and Sunday.
